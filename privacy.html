Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who we are

Tamnan Nihon is a personal publishing tool operated by the creator of the TikTok account @tamnan_nihon ("we").

What the tool does

The tool uploads videos that we created ourselves to our own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. It is not offered to other users.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information of our own TikTok account (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account a video is posted to.
  • An access token issued by TikTok, used only to publish our own videos.

We do not collect, read, store or share data about any other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

Storage and security

The access token is stored on the operator's own computer and in a private database that only the operator can access. It is never shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

We keep the token only while the tool is in use. Revoking access in TikTok settings, or contacting us, deletes it.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact us via direct message to @tamnan_nihon on TikTok or Threads.
