Tamnan Nihon (ห้องสมุดตำนานญี่ปุ่น)

Tamnan Nihon is a small publishing tool used by the creator of the Thai-language account @tamnan_nihon. It turns short, source-cited stories about local Japanese legends, folk gods and festivals into one-minute vertical videos with Thai narration and subtitles, and publishes them to the creator's own accounts.

The tool is used only by its operator to post to the operator's own TikTok account. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not access other users' content.
